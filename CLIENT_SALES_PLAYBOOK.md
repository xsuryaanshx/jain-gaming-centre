## 1. The Core Business Model & Unfair Advantage

Traditional agencies pitch with PowerPoint decks, PDFs, and generic promises. Clients are skeptical because every salesperson says the same thing.

### 🎯 Your "Trojan Horse" Strategy: _Spec Work as Live Demonstration_

Instead of pitching an idea, **you build the working prototype first** using the AMI Website Factory:

- You spend 45–60 minutes spinning up a high-performance, animated site with their real business name, actual Google reviews, and real product images.
- You send them a live GitHub Pages / Cloudflare link directly on WhatsApp.
- **The Psychology:** They see their own shop, their own name, and their own five-star reviews shining on their smartphone screen. It triggers an instant psychological sense of ownership. They don't want to lose it.

## 2. Pricing Matrix & Tiered Packages

Do not offer a one-size-fits-all price. Quote according to the **business type and ticket size of their customers**.

| Package Tier                       | Target Business Type                                             | One-Time Setup Fee     | Annual Maintenance (Hosting + Care) | Estimated Monthly Equivalent |
| :--------------------------------- | :--------------------------------------------------------------- | :--------------------- | :---------------------------------- | :--------------------------- |
| **Tier 1: Starter / Retail Store** | Gaming lounges, cafes, local boutiques, salons, repair shops     | **₹10,000 – ₹15,000**  | **₹5,000 / year**                   | ~₹400 / mo                   |
| **Tier 2: B2B & Manufacturing**    | Kitchen hardware, fabrication, packaging, machinery distributors | **₹20,000 – ₹30,000**  | **₹6,000 – ₹8,000 / year**          | ~₹500–₹650 / mo              |
| **Tier 3: Luxury & Professional**  | Architects, interior design studios, premium clinics, builders   | **₹35,000 – ₹50,000+** | **₹10,000 – ₹15,000 / year**        | ~₹850–₹1,250 / mo            |

---

### What Each Fee Covers

#### A. One-Time Setup Fee Covers:

- Custom design with brand colors, typography, and logo integration.
- Mobile-first responsive optimization (90%+ of their visitors use mobile).
- Fast micro-animations, product filtering, and smooth transitions.
- Click-to-WhatsApp and Click-to-Call direct integration.
- Google Reviews embed & social proof showcase.
- On-page local SEO (Google Maps meta tags, title tags, schema markup).
- Domain mapping (linking their `.com` or `.in` domain to cloud hosting).

#### B. Annual Care & Hosting Plan (₹5,000/yr) Covers:

- **High-Speed Cloud Hosting:** Global CDN with 99.99% uptime.
- **SSL Security Lock:** Automated HTTPS certificate.
- **On-Demand WhatsApp Updates:** _"Any time you get a new product or change prices, WhatsApp me the photo and I update it within 24 hours."_
- **Seasonal / Festival Banners:** Diwali, New Year, Independence Day offers.
- **Technical Peace of Mind:** Zero server management for the owner.

## 3. The Psychology of Quoting & Anchoring

### правило #1: Never Quote First on the Phone

If an owner asks on the first phone call: _"Beta kharcha kitna aayega?"_:

> **Your Response:**  
> _"Uncle/Sir, paise ki aap chinta mat kijiye. Pehle aap phone pe live website chala ke dekhiye. Agar aapko lage ki haan isse meri dukaan ka standard aur customer reach badhega, tabhi hum baith ke reasonable baat karenge. Main store pe aake aapse milta hoon."_

### правило #2: Price Anchoring (When Forced to Quote)

Always anchor against agency market rates before revealing your price:

1. **Market Standard Anchor:** _"Normal IT agencies charge ₹30,000 to ₹45,000 for custom-coded, high-speed interactive websites."_
2. **Local / Relationship Position:** _"Lekin kyunki hum direct Indore se hain aur local businesses ko empower kar rahe hain..."_
3. **The Offer:** _"...humne isko flat ₹10,000 – ₹12,000 setup aur ₹5,000 saal ka worry-free maintenance rakha hai."_
4. **ROI Justification:** _"Aapka bas 1 ya 2 naye customer ke order se poora website ka kharcha recover ho jaata hai."_

## 6. Payment Terms, Milestones & Revision Policy

### 💳 The 50 / 50 Standard Milestone Model

Never do 100% post-delivery, and never ask 100% upfront:

```
┌─────────────────────────────────┐       ┌─────────────────────────────────┐
│        50% Advance Token        │ ────▶ │     50% Balance Upon Launch     │
│   (e.g., ₹5,000 - ₹7,500)       │       │    (On Official Domain)         │
│ Paid to initiate customization  │       │ Paid after final testing & live │
└─────────────────────────────────┘       └─────────────────────────────────┘
```

- **Advance Token:** Confirms their commitment, covers domain verification, and reserves their development slot.
- **Final Balance:** Paid the day the website goes live on their official `.com` or `.in` domain and is approved by them.

### ⏱️ Delivery Timeline: Always Quote 3 to 5 Days

Even if the prototype is already finished:

- **Never say:** _"I can launch it in 1 hour."_ (Devalues the work).
- **Always say:** _"Basic architecture ready hai, par aapki custom rate list, domain linking, aur mobile testing mein 3 se 4 working days lagte hain."_

### 🔄 Revision Policy

- **Pre-Launch:** **2 Rounds of Consolidation Revisions** (Client reviews all photos, phone numbers, addresses, and content in two structured passes).
- **Post-Launch:** Unlimited minor updates (prices, new photos, banners) covered under their **Annual Care Plan**.

## 8. Land-and-Expand: Upselling Existing Clients

Once a client is paying you ₹5,000/year for maintenance, you have established deep trust. In 3 to 6 months, pitch these natural upgrades:

### 📈 Upsell 1: Google My Business (GMB) Optimization (₹3,000 – ₹5,000 One-Time)

- Optimize their Google Maps ranking with keyword-rich titles, categories, business hours, and geotagged store photos.
- Connect website link to their GMB profile for local SEO boost.

### 📈 Upsell 2: WhatsApp Business Catalog Setup (₹2,500 One-Time)

- Format their top 20 products directly inside WhatsApp Business with prices, descriptions, and direct links to their website product pages.

### 📈 Upsell 3: QR Code Physical Collateral (₹1,500 – ₹3,000)

- Design custom branded QR code standees / acrylic table tents for their billing counter: _"Scan to browse our full online catalog & latest arrivals."_
